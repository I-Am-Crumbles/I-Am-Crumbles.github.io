# Camera Equipment Inventory

This is planning/reference material for the Photography section, not a published post yet.

Complete inventory of gear, with the best scenarios, camera pairings, and settings to use for
each one when building out the portfolio.

## Camera bodies & standalone devices

- **Canon EOS Rebel T7**
  Ideal situation: planned, high-detail photoshoots in good lighting.
  Best used for: studio or outdoor portraits, detailed close-ups, landscapes.

- **Canon Rebel 40D**
  Ideal situation: high-speed action, sports, or erratic movement outdoors.
  Best used for: dogs running, local sporting events, shooting in unpredictable/dusty weather.

- **Canon PowerShot G7 X Mark II**
  Ideal situation: casual social events, vacations, low-light indoor storytelling where a DSLR
  is too bulky.
  Best used for: vlogging, dinner out with friends, concerts, street photography.

- **Samsung Galaxy S20**
  Ideal situation: instant convenience, extreme high-contrast lighting, ultra-wide
  architecture, night scenes.
  Best used for: high-contrast sunsets, dark environments (Night Mode), macro textures using
  the 2x zoom trick.

- **Canon PowerShot SX160 IS**
  Ideal situation: distant subjects on bright days where you can't physically get closer.
  Best used for: stadium concerts/graduations from the back row, backyard birdwatching.

- **Canon PowerShot SD780is**
  (listed in inventory, no scenario notes yet)

## Canon lenses (interchangeable between T7 and 40D)

- **EF 50mm — "The Portrait Master"**
  Best camera pairing: Rebel T7 (to leverage the 24.1MP resolution).
  Ideal situation: headshots, couples' portraits, isolating a single subject from a busy
  background.
  Why: razor-sharp subject with a beautifully melted, blurry background (bokeh).

- **EF-S 55-250mm — "The Action Telephoto"**
  Best camera pairing: Rebel 40D (to leverage the 6.5 frames-per-second burst speed).
  Ideal situation: dogs playing across the yard, local soccer games, wildlife.
  Why: safely brings far-away action up close and compresses the background beautifully.

- **EF-S 24mm — "The Compact 'Pancake' Lens"**
  Best camera pairing: either body (perfect for making the heavy 40D lighter).
  Ideal situation: street photography, casual environmental portraits, urban exploring.
  Why: incredibly tiny, sharp, and lightweight — makes the DSLR look less intimidating to
  people on the street.

- **EF-S 18-55mm — "The Versatile Do-It-All"**
  Best camera pairing: Rebel T7.
  Ideal situation: sweeping landscapes, group family photos, standard daytime travel.
  Why: flexibility to zoom out wide for a broad view or zoom in slightly for a standard frame.
